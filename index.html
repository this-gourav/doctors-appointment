Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to book an appointment in advance? +
While walk-ins are sometimes accommodated, we strongly recommend booking in advance to minimize your wait time. You can book easily via WhatsApp, our online form, or through the AI Call Agent.
What should I bring to my first visit? +
Please bring any previous prescriptions, test reports, your Aadhaar card or photo ID, and a list of medications you are currently taking. This helps us give you faster, more accurate care.
Is the clinic open on Sundays? +
We are open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays. For emergencies, please call our number which is attended by our duty staff.
Do you offer telemedicine or online consultations? +
Yes! We offer WhatsApp-based consultations for follow-up visits and minor queries. For new patients or complex conditions, an in-person visit is recommended for proper examination. Video calls can be arranged on request.
How does the AI Call Agent work? +
When you click "Request a Call", an AI voice agent will call your number within minutes. It will ask for your name, preferred appointment date, and the reason for your visit — then schedule a confirmed slot and notify our clinic staff automatically.
